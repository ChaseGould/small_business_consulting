Frequently Asked Questions

Do we have to replace the systems we already use?

No. Most of our work makes your existing systems work better together. Your scheduling platform, payroll, and spreadsheets stay; we build the connections and tools around them so your team stops doing the manual work between them.

How long does a build take?

A first working version of one workflow typically ships in 2 to 6 weeks, depending on scope and how many systems are involved. You see progress on real data early, not a big reveal at the end.

How is pricing structured?

A fixed fee for the initial build, then a monthly rate that covers hosting, monitoring, support, and ongoing changes. No surprise hourly invoices.

Is our data safe?

Yes, and we treat this as non-negotiable. Least-privilege access, employee and customer personal data kept out of logs and third-party services, and dry runs before anything writes to a live system, especially anything payroll-adjacent.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any business where people spend hours on repetitive manual work: scheduling, data entry between systems, reports, follow-ups, paperwork. That shows up in security, staffing, field services, healthcare, retail, and plenty of others. The workflow matters more than the industry.

What if we're not sure what to automate?

Start with whatever your team re-types, re-copies, or chases every week. Tell us about it in two sentences and we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth automating and what we'd do first.
